How you get it

Pre-order starts with A, so A is the root.
In-order splits at A:
left subtree: C, B, E, D
right subtree: F, G
Next in pre-order is B, so B is the left child of A. In-order around B is C | B | E, D, so C is left of B, and E, D is right of B.
After B and C, pre-order has D, so D is the right child of B. In-order around D is E | D, so E is left of D.
Right of A is F. In-order is F, G, so G is right of F.
